\begin{table}[!h]
  \caption{Returns Around Alternative Structural Break Dates}\label{tab:samp-split}
  \small
  
  The table reports mean long-short returns before and after three candidate break dates: the end of each paper's original sample period, December 2004 following \citet{chen2022zeroing}, and a break date estimated separately for each predictor. Estimated break dates minimize the residual sum of squares from a one-break constant-mean model (\citealt{bai1998estimating}). Mean date is the average break date across predictors; \% of signals with decay is the share with a lower mean return after the break.
